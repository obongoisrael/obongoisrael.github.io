Why do customers leave, and who leaves next?

Customer Churn Analysis

Found the main drivers of churn across 7,000 telecom customers with SQL, then scored every customer by churn risk with a logistic regression to give a retention team a focused call list.

Key findings

  • Overall churn is 26.1%. Month-to-month customers churn at 40.7%, against 2.6% on two-year contracts.
  • First-year customers churn at 48.4%. After four years, only 3.4% do.
  • Recommended annual-plan offers, onboarding check-ins and auto-pay over electronic checks.

  • pandas
  • scikit-learn

Where do Kenyan households pay the most for food?

Kenya Food Price Trends, 2021–2025

Combined 10 messy county files (4,730 raw rows) of monthly prices for 8 staple foods into one clean table, then analysed trends, county gaps, seasonality and volatility.

Key findings

  • 2023 was the peak for every staple. By 2025 prices were still about 19% above 2021.
  • Maize peaks in June, before the long-rains harvest, and is cheapest in January.
  • Built an Excel workbook of live formulas with dropdowns for any commodity, county or year.

How fast is revenue growing, and who drives it?

E-commerce Sales Performance

Analysed three years of online sales (105,000 orders across four related tables) with SQL, and presented the results as a one-page executive report and a Looker Studio dashboard.

Key findings

  • Black Friday season (November–December) brings 26% of yearly revenue.
  • 2,659 frequent buyers hadn't ordered in about 9 months, holding 15% of revenue.
  • Used JOINs, CTEs, LAG() for growth and NTILE() for RFM customer segments.
  • SQL
  • Window functions
  • RFM
